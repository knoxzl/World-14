# Coal generator

Craft in the G menu with 20 steel sheets and 5 cable. Anchor the generator over an MV cable using a wrench, insert coal, then start it through its interface or interaction verb.

It supplies 10 kW on MV only. Each coal provides approximately 60 seconds at full output; capacity is 30 coal. Fuel consumption continues while running even without demand. Only coal is accepted. The standard engine hum plays while running and stops when switched off or out of fuel. Construction does not include starting fuel.

Balance and recipe are in `Resources/Prototypes/_Planet14/coal_generator.yml`. Artwork is Knoxzl's original 32x32 sprite.
